## 2026-10-13: 1.2 move types and type chart (run 11)
- Type chart in `game/engine/types.ts`: eight super-effective (2×) matchups, each with a short OS reason shown after the hit. Interrupts preempt the scheduler (io > scheduler), preemption (scheduler > process), lock-holder preemption (scheduler > sync), thrashing (process > memory), the page cache (memory > storage), swapping (storage > process), locks serialise racers (sync > process), and a semaphore guards a device (sync > io). The reverse of each matchup is resisted (½×). Everything else is neutral. Helpers: `effectiveness`, `effectMessage`, `superReason`.
- Damage and reducer: `moveDamage` takes an `effect` multiplier and `counterDamage(level, effect)`. `Fighter` has an optional `type`. The `answer` action carries `moveType` and `counterType`, and outcomes record `effect`. Untyped creatures take neutral damage. The player's Bytling stays untyped until starter choice (1.3), so boss counters keep their tested balance.
- Battle UI: the foe's HP box shows a type tag on its top edge. The move panel shows `×2` / `×0.5` against the current foe. After a hit, "It's super effective!" is followed by the reason, and "It's not very effective…" appears on resisted hits. A typed player gets the same lines on counters.
- Content: bosses take their gym's type, and Glitchit is a process type (so the demo's CONTEXT SWAP is super effective). Two boss moves were retyped where the OS fact fits: RR's "Timer Tick" (the quantum is enforced by a timer interrupt, io vs Chronogolem) and "Thrash Storm" (process vs Thrashmoth). The interview gauntlet's mixed types now matter too.
